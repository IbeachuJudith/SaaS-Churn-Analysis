**Business Problem**

Scale Flow was growing fast, but customers were leaving faster than before. Churn rose 13.76% year-over-year, and 2025 was the worst year on record. Leadership needed to know where customers were leaving, why, and what to fix first.

This project analyzes three years of SaaS business data to find the root causes of churn across customer segments, acquisition channels, regions, industries, and payment plans, then turns the findings into prioritized recommendations for the next 6 months.

**Tools Used**
Excel | Power Query | Power BI

**Data & Preparation**
The dataset covers customer records, revenue, payment plans, acquisition channels, regions, and industry segments. I cleaned and transformed the data in Power Query and built calculated fields to measure churn rate, revenue contribution, and year-over-year growth across every segment.

**Key Insights**
1. Enterprise is the biggest revenue risk. It generates the most revenue but has a 29% churn rate.
2. Strong acquisition, weak retention. Referral and Paid Ads bring in the most customers but also see the highest churn.
3. Regional split. Port Harcourt and Enugu deliver strong revenue with low churn despite smaller customer bases, while Lagos has high churn despite strong volume.
4. Healthcare is a concentrated risk. It is the highest-revenue industry, but its 29% churn rate puts that revenue in danger.
5. Churn is business-wide. Every region, channel, and payment plan shows elevated churn, so this is a systemic problem rather than one weak segment.

**Recommendations**
1. Protect high-value revenue in Enterprise and Healthcare with stronger onboarding and early churn-signal monitoring.
2. Fix core retention across all plans by improving engagement and onboarding, with a target of bringing overall churn below 20%.
3. Scale what works by investing more in Education, Port Harcourt, Enugu, and Social Media, where retention is strongest.
4. Monetize existing demand by upselling Free and Basic users to Pro and Enterprise plans.
